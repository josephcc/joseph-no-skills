# Credits for the images in this folder

Every screenshot here is of a **synthetic study** — the "Skim study" demo folder, with invented
participants and invented transcripts. No real participant, transcript, or recording appears in
any of them.

`frame-peek.png` shows the workbench's frame peek, which needs a recording's frame grid beside the
transcript. The demo has no recording, so the frames behind that screenshot were built from:

> *Scholia and scientometrics with Wikidata — Figures 2 and 4*, by Finn Årup Nielsen,
> Daniel Mietchen and Egon Willighagen (2017). Dedicated to the public domain under
> [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
> <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Scholia_and_scientometrics_with_Wikidata_-_Figure_4.png>

CC0 asks for nothing in return; the credit is here so the provenance of every pixel in this folder
is on the record.
